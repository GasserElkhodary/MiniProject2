# Reflection: stan-dev_rstanarm

rstanarm's longest gap (Aug 2013–May 2015, 22 months) is a pre-launch pause. A two-week prototype by Ben Goodrich in July 2013 was set aside while Stan and rstan matured. It was easy to interpret from the commit messages. The recovery is unmistakable: on 2015-06-30 Jonah Gabry committed the core package piece by piece (stan models, priors, stan_lm/stan_glm, S3 methods, docs), leading to the 2016 peak. Recovery was driven by a new contributor joined by the original author. After the peak, activity steadily declined into maintenance, with four ≥3-month gaps. The latest commits fix deprecated ggplot2 syntax and CI.
